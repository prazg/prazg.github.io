Research & Educational Open-Source Software — Non-SaMD Notice

All web applications, volumetric & RANO calculators, stereotactic planners, molecular classifiers, clinical trial search heuristics, and foundation model runners hosted across prazg.github.io are provided strictly for academic research, algorithmic benchmarking, and medical education. They are not certified Software as a Medical Device (Non-SaMD) and must not be used for primary clinical diagnosis, surgical navigation on patients, or unsupervised treatment decisions. Browser-local sandboxes process NIfTI/DICOM data entirely in local memory with zero external upload.
