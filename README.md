## Overview

`prazg.github.io` is the zero-build, 100% self-contained, WCAG 2.1 AA accessible central observatory indexing Dr Prajwal Ghimire's open-source clinical research web applications, radiology & pathology foundation model portals, translational glioblastoma radiogenomics frameworks, and public-interest multi-hazard observatories.

### Ecosystem Highlights
- **10 Live Clinical & AI Web Apps** across neurosurgery, neuroimaging, neuro-oncology, and computational pathology
- **99 Curated Open Foundation Models** (*28 Brain Radiology · 55 Clinical/Preclinical Histology · 16 Rodent/GEMM Animal Pathology*)
- **44 Indexed Open Neuro Datasets & MICCAI Benchmarks** via OpenNeuroAtlas
- **100% Self-Contained Architecture:** Zero external CDN `<script>`/`<link>`/font/image dependencies, zero analytics trackers, seamless Dark & Light themes, keyboard-accessible search (`/` & `Escape`), interactive 6-workflow decision router, and 1-click Markdown/BibTeX directory export

## 2. Radiogenomics & Glioblastoma Research Frameworks

- **[DECIPHER-GBM](https://github.com/prazg/DECIPHER-GBM):** Learning glioblastoma immune phenotypes from whole-brain MRI using a hybrid deep learning radiogenomic framework.
- **[PRECISE_GBM](https://github.com/prazg/PRECISE_GBM):** Predictive Radiomics for Evaluation of Cancer Immune Signature in Glioblastoma integrating MRI radiomics, genomics, and immune signatures.
- **[PRECISE-GAN](https://github.com/prazg/PRECISE-GAN):** Synthetic FLAIR brain MRI generation pipeline using GANs.
- **[Deep-Features-Nifti-Network-DEN](https://github.com/prazg/Deep-Features-Nifti-Network-DEN):** Deep Extraction Network (CNN) extracting deep volumetric features from NIfTI MRI scans of segmented glioblastomas.
- **[NeuroOnc_PROMs](https://github.com/prazg/NeuroOnc_PROMs):** Patient-reported outcome and experience measures (PROMs/PREMs) for neuro-oncology patients developed at King's College Hospital, London.

## 3. Independent Nepal Open-Science & Multi-Hazard Portals

- **[Nepal TRAYA](https://prazg.github.io/Nepal_TRAYA/)** ([Repo](https://github.com/prazg/Nepal_TRAYA)): Multi-Hazard Early Warning System for earthquakes, floods, and landslides across Nepal.
- **[Nepal Quake Watch](https://prazg.github.io/Nepal_Quake_Watch/)** ([Repo](https://github.com/prazg/Nepal_Quake_Watch)): Live earthquake monitoring integrating USGS + NEMRC catalogues, ShakeMap intensity, and ward-level population/hospital exposure.
- **[Nepal Cryosphere Inventory](https://prazg.github.io/Nepal_Cryosphere_Inventory/)** ([Repo](https://github.com/prazg/Nepal_Cryosphere_Inventory)): Open, rebuildable inventory of 4,480 glaciers and 1,429 glacial lakes in Nepal with ice velocity and outburst flood records.
- **[Nepal Heritage Catalogue](https://prazg.github.io/Nepal_Heritage_Catalogue/)** ([Repo](https://github.com/prazg/Nepal_Heritage_Catalogue)): Searchable catalogue of Nepalese cultural objects, manuscripts, and archives held outside Nepal with repatriation tracking.
- **[HMA GLOF Dashboard](https://prazg.github.io/GLOF_dashboard/)** ([Repo](https://github.com/prazg/GLOF_dashboard)): Interactive review of glacial lake outburst floods across High Mountain Asia.
